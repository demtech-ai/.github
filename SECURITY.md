# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities by email to **eng-admin@demtech.ai**. Do not open a public GitHub issue.

Include:
- what is affected (repository, service or URL)
- steps to reproduce
- the impact you expect

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within 48 hours.
- We keep you updated while we investigate and fix it.
- We credit you once the fix is released, if you want.

Please give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it publicly.
